# Review Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to bounded research artifact reviews.

## Decision

Reviewers record whether an artifact is accepted, revised, or quarantined.
Every review decision should be clear, fair, and supported by observable evidence.

## Exceptions

Urgent operational notes may use an abbreviated review when the exception is recorded.
