## Knowledge and limitations

The roadmap sequences integrity, baseline, external validity, context, security, human review, retrieval, and routing with useful decision gates. Timelines, statistical power, reviewer capacity, and operational budgets remain unspecified.

## Five highest-value opportunities

2. **RFR-002:** Run a contained repeated baseline (Phase 1).
3. **RFR-004:** Establish external validity (Phase 2).
4. **RFR-005:** Compare context/state policies (Phase 3).
5. **RFR-007:** Compare human-review policies (Phase 5).

**Challenge:** The linear phase framing understates feedback loops; failed grader validation must send tasks back to contract design rather than advance automatically.
