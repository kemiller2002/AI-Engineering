# Independent pilot integrity review

**Opportunity:** Obtain blinded, independent construct and probe labels for ET-004-P01 and ET-014-P01.

**Background and unknowns:** Designer calibration cannot establish construct validity. It is unknown whether independent experts agree that the tasks are answerable, representative, leakage-resistant, and correctly graded.

**Origin and evidence:** `research/evaluation/NEXT-AGENT-START-HERE.md` (“Current Blocker”); `13-independent-review-protocol.md`; `07-results.md` (“Scope Warning”, “Failed/Unexecuted Tests”); EV-E014.

**Dependencies:** External reviewers with no role in task design.

**Suggested REP/method:** Cycle 004, preregister reviewer materials, collect labels independently, measure agreement, adjudicate only after capture, then validate/revise/quarantine each pilot.

**Outputs and success:** Immutable reviews, agreement table, adjudication record, and pilot disposition. Success requires two independent reviews per pilot and no concealed disagreement.

**Recommended agent:** Research coordinator plus independent domain reviewers. **Effort:** small. **Expected gain:** converts the main validity assumption into evidence and gates all baseline work.
