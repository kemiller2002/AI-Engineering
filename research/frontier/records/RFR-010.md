# Knowledge-graph value experiment

**Opportunity:** Test whether a maintained research graph improves retrieval, handoff accuracy, duplicate detection, and research selection enough to justify its cost.

**Unknowns:** Causal value, maintenance burden, ontology stability, and graph-vs-search advantage.

**Origin and evidence:** `CURRENT-STATE.md` open question; `knowledge-platform/knowledge-genome.json`; `Repository-Architecture.md`; `Search-Architecture.md`; this analysis’s dependency graph.

**Dependencies:** RFR-004 and RFR-009; stable corpus and identifiers.

**Method:** Predefine representative navigation and synthesis tasks; compare file/search baseline with graph-assisted workflow; measure correctness, time, missed provenance, duplicate proposals, and maintenance time.

**Outputs and success:** Minimal graph prototype, benchmark tasks, cost-benefit decision. Success requires material, replicated gains that exceed ongoing maintenance cost.

**Recommended agent:** Knowledge-engineering researcher. **Effort:** medium-large. **Expected gain:** potentially compounding research coordination; high ontology-debt risk.
