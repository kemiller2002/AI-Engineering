# Risk-triggered human escalation study

**Opportunity:** Compare fixed approval gates, risk-triggered escalation, and end-only review.

**Unknowns:** Best intervention timing, reviewer burden, missed-risk rate, autonomy loss, and trust calibration.

**Origin and evidence:** `AI-Knowledge-Gap-Analysis.md` human-agent collaboration row; `AI-Research-Roadmap.md` Phase 5; `12-research-and-engineering-roadmap.md` Workstream D; `10-threat-model.md`.

**Dependencies:** RFR-004 and RFR-008; ethically bounded tasks and trained reviewers.

**Method:** Within-task policy comparison with explicit risk strata; measure verified outcomes, preventions, false alarms, review minutes, and recovery.

**Outputs and success:** Escalation policy, burden/risk frontier, failure analysis. Success requires lower consequential-risk exposure without unacceptable verified-outcome loss.

**Recommended agent:** Human-factors researcher with safety reviewer. **Effort:** large. **Expected gain:** evidence for controlled delegation.
